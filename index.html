Work

CrashDiag

A mechanically verified infrastructure-diagnosis RL environment. Agents repair faults through bounded JSON actions, and rewards come from executable sandbox checks and state-based tests instead of LLM judges. It is the first environment from Indium AI Labs.

  • Bounded actions
